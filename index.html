About Us

Bright Beginnings Learning Foundation was founded in 2022 by local teachers and community volunteers who were concerned about learners falling behind in their studies due to overcrowded classrooms, limited resources, and lack of academic support at home.

What began as informal study sessions in a community hall has grown into a structured tutoring programme that partners with local schools to identify and support learners who need help the most.

Mission

Provide free, accessible, and consistent tutoring and homework support in a safe and encouraging environment.

Vision

A community where every child, regardless of financial circumstances, has access to the academic support needed to succeed.

Community

Serving learners, parents, guardians, and volunteer tutors across Lebowakgomo and surrounding areas.

Our Programmes

Primary School Support

Homework assistance and tutoring for Grades 4-7 in Mathematics, English, Natural Sciences, and other core subjects.

High School Tutoring

Focused support for Grades 8-12 including exam preparation, study skills, and subject-specific revision sessions.

Study Skills

Time management, note-taking, reading strategies, and confidence-building workshops for learners.
